FAQ

Common questions

How does payment work?

Classes are billed monthly. For courses with a listed price, payment is handled securely online through Paystack. For other courses, your tutor will confirm the monthly fee directly with you and provide an access code once payment is received. Access renews each month — you'll be notified when it's time to renew.

What if my child misses a class?

Reach out to us and we'll help arrange a catch-up or reschedule where possible.

Is my child's class safe and supervised?

Yes — every class is led live by a real tutor, not an open chatroom. No child attends unsupervised.

Can students join from outside Nigeria?

Yes — classes are scheduled with time zones in mind, so students abroad can join comfortably.
