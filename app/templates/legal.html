Terms & Privacy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email, phone number, and your child's name and class level, so we can enroll them in the right course and communicate with you about classes and payments.

How we use it

Your information is used only to manage your child's enrollment, process payments, send confirmation emails, and coordinate class scheduling with your tutor. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Payments

Card payments are processed securely through Paystack. We do not store your card details on our servers.

Classes & scheduling

Once enrolled and paid, class scheduling is coordinated directly with your tutor via WhatsApp.

Your data, your control

You can request that your account and your child's information be removed at any time by contacting us directly.

Contact

Questions about your data or these terms? Reach out to us on WhatsApp or by email.
